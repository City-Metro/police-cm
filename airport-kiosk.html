Airport Metro Transit Center Kiosk

A joint kiosk staffed by both City Metro Transit Police and FLX Police, positioned where the rail and bus concourse meets the airport connector to Felix Lego City International Airport.

Kiosk — Joint Transit/FLX
Location

Airport Metro Transit Center, arrivals-level connector to Felix Lego City International Airport.

Staffing

Staffed jointly: CMTP officers cover the transit center itself, while FLX Police staff take over jurisdiction once you cross into airport property.

Hours

Staffed during all scheduled transit-center service hours, aligned with flight and train schedules.

About this location

Where two agencies meet

This kiosk sits right at the jurisdictional handoff point: City Metro Transit Police officers here handle the rail platforms, bus bays, and transit center itself, while FLX Police — a separate agency under the CMTP umbrella — take over as the primary law enforcement agency once you're on airport grounds.

For travelers, that handoff is invisible day to day: both agencies staff the kiosk together, share information, and coordinate directly so a safety concern raised anywhere in the transit center reaches the right officers immediately.

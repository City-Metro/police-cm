Why now

We're staffing every platform, systemwide.

The Platform Presence Program means at least two officers at every rail station in Lego City County, and at least two per platform at major hubs. That's the largest expansion in CMTP's history — and it means real openings across patrol, dispatch, and specialized units, not just at headquarters.

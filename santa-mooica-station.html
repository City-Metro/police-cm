Downtown Santa Mooica Station

A full police station serving Downtown Santa Mooica Station and the surrounding coastal district, one of the westernmost anchors of the CMTP system.

Police Station
Location

Downtown Santa Mooica Station, street level.

Staffing

Staffed around the clock, with platform officers assigned under the Platform Presence Program.

Hours

Open 24 hours. Front counter staffed for walk-ins during all shifts.

About this location

Anchoring the coastal end of the line

Downtown Santa Mooica Station sits at the western end of the rail network, and its station house handles the full range of CMTP work for the district: platform patrol, incident response, and coordination with the nearby Santa Mooica Recreation Area kiosk during high-traffic weekends.

Because the surrounding district draws heavy weekend and event ridership, this station works closely with the recreation area kiosk to keep coverage consistent along the whole corridor, from the platform to the Sports Center.

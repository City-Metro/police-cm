Santa Mooica Recreation Area Kiosk

A transit police kiosk positioned in the Santa Mooica State Recreation Area, right next to the City Metro Sports Center — covering one of the system's busiest recreational destinations.

Kiosk
Location

Santa Mooica State Recreation Area, adjacent to the City Metro Sports Center entrance.

Staffing

Staffed during recreation area operating hours, with additional officers on peak event days at the Sports Center.

Hours

Aligned with State Recreation Area hours; extended staffing during Sports Center events.

About this location

Covering the recreation corridor

This kiosk exists because of how much foot traffic moves between transit, the recreation area, and the City Metro Sports Center — especially on event days. Officers here focus on the shared trail and plaza space connecting the rail stop to the recreation area, and coordinate with Sports Center event staff and City Metro Transit Security when large crowds are expected.

It's a lighter-footprint kiosk than a full station house, built for a location where the job is as much about presence and wayfinding help as it is enforcement.

How the system is secured

Divisions & Jurisdiction

City Metro Transit Police is the primary law enforcement agency for Lego City County's rail and bus systems. Two other groups work alongside CMTP to keep the system safe — here's how the responsibility is divided.

Primary jurisdiction

City Metro Transit Police

CMTP is responsible for securing all of Lego City County's transit systems, including rail and bus. As the systemwide law enforcement agency, CMTP officers hold full arrest authority and jurisdiction across every train, bus, platform, and transit facility City Metro operates — with more than 1,000 officers on duty across the county.

Systemwide initiative

The Platform Presence Program

Every rail station in the system now has at least two City Metro Transit Police officers assigned to it. Major hubs — the stations where multiple lines meet or ridership is highest — are staffed with at least two officers per platform, not just per station.

Security division

City Metro Transit Security

Alongside sworn transit police, City Metro also operates City Metro Transit Security — a division responsible for securing City Metro events, City Metro's administrative divisions, and Union Station. Transit Security staff work in close coordination with CMTP, providing an additional layer of presence at high-traffic and high-attendance locations.

Separate agency

FLX Police

Felix Lego City International Airport is secured by FLX Police, a separate law enforcement agency that operates under the CMTP umbrella. FLX Police officers are the primary law enforcement presence at the airport, distinct from CMTP's rail and bus jurisdiction.

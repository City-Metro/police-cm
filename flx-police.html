A separate agency under CMTP

FLX Police

FLX Police secures Felix Lego City International Airport. It's a distinct law enforcement agency from City Metro Transit Police, operating under the CMTP umbrella.

What FLX Police does

Primary law enforcement at FLX

FLX Police is responsible for providing security and serves as the primary law enforcement agency for Felix Lego City International Airport. That includes everything from terminal patrol and access control to responding to incidents anywhere on airport property.

FLX Police is organized as a separate agency from City Metro Transit Police — it isn't a division of CMTP the way Transit Security is. Instead, it operates under the broader CMTP umbrella, which allows the two agencies to share training standards, dispatch coordination, and resources while each keeps its own distinct jurisdiction.

Where FLX meets CMTP

The Airport Metro Transit Center kiosk

The clearest place to see how these two agencies work together is the Airport Metro Transit Center, where a joint Transit/FLX Police kiosk sits right at the boundary between the rail system and the airport. CMTP handles the transit center; FLX Police takes over on airport grounds.

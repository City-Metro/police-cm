Lego City County · Transit Public Safety

Every platform. Every train. Every rider.

City Metro Transit Police (CMTP) is the sworn law enforcement agency responsible for rail, bus, and transit-facility safety across Lego City County — backed by more than 1,000 officers on duty across the system.

Systemwide coverage

One agency, the whole transit system.

CMTP holds primary law enforcement jurisdiction over every rail line, bus route, station, and transit facility that City Metro operates. Officers patrol platforms, ride trains, staff substations, and respond countywide — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

New initiative

The Platform Presence Program

CMTP has begun staffing at least two transit police officers at every rail station, systemwide — with major hubs assigning at least two officers per platform. It's the largest single expansion of visible policing in the department's history, and it's already underway.

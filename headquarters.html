CMTP · HQ

Transit Police Headquarters

CMTP's command center, located at Downtown Lego City Station. Headquarters houses dispatch, department administration, and the office of the Chief of Transit Police.

Headquarters
Location

Downtown Lego City Station, administrative wing.

Staffing

Home base for CMTP command staff, systemwide dispatch, and records — plus a full complement of platform and station officers on-site.

Hours

Public records and administrative offices open standard business hours. Dispatch and station patrol operate 24/7.

About this location

Where the department is run

Headquarters is the operational center of CMTP: systemwide dispatch is based here, coordinating every officer across the rail and bus network, and it's where new policy, training, and hiring decisions are made. Downtown Lego City Station itself is one of the system's major hubs, so headquarters staff work alongside a full contingent of platform officers, not behind a desk removed from the system they run.

Records requests, community liaison inquiries, and media requests for CMTP are all routed through headquarters.
